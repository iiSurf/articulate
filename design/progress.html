Articulate: the boards, and the practice game

Rough drafts. The words and numbers are made up. The Progress tab holds three boards: Top words, Needs work, and Most improved. Under them, the sounds that cause the trouble lead to a short practice game.

The boards are personal: they rank your friend's own words against each other. Everything stays on their phone, so nobody else can be on them.

The three boards

Each word gets a score from 0 to 100 from its colored parts: green counts fully, yellow mostly, orange a little, red nothing.
