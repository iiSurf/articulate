Articulate: Ukraine themes

Round two. iiSurf picked seven of the ten: 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, and 10. All seven go into the app, and changing between them is one tap.

How changing works: the "UA → EN" tag in the band opens Your colors. It sets your first language (Ukrainian), and the colors come with it, so a learner from another country would get their own set. You choose a look for the day and one for night, and Articulate switches when your phone switches to dark mode. To start with, the day look is Sky over wheat and the night look is Night flag.

The four grade colors are the same in every theme, so they always mean the same thing. Each also has its own mark:

  • good
  • close
  • a little off
  • needs work
