Ukrainian tips to review

These are short tips that help someone who speaks Ukrainian make English sounds, by starting from a Ukrainian sound they already have. They were written by a program, not by a Ukrainian speaker, and have never been checked.

Please read as a native speaker. For each row, mark OK or write what is wrong: a Ukrainian sound that isn't really close, a word that sounds unnatural, or a sentence that would confuse.

th as in thinkthank, thin, three, thing, think, thick с (сам) Start from the Ukrainian с (as in сам), then slide the tip of your tongue forward between your teeth. Blow air softly, with no buzz.
  • You said s: your tongue stayed behind your teeth. Move the tip forward until it touches the edge of your top teeth.
  • You said t: your tongue stopped the air. Let it rest lightly between your teeth and keep the air flowing.
  • You said d: your tongue stopped the air and buzzed. This th has no buzz, just soft air between your teeth.
  • You said f: your lip touched your teeth. Keep your lips apart and put the tongue between your teeth.

  • You said d: your tongue tapped behind your teeth. Let it stay between your teeth, and let the air buzz through.
  • You said z: your tongue stayed behind your teeth. Move the tip forward, between your teeth.

  • You said v: your teeth touched your lip. Keep them apart, and round your lips like for у.

  • You said w: your lips were round. Put your top teeth on your lower lip and let the air buzz.

r as in redred, right, road, run, rice, ready р (рука) Don't roll or tap it like the Ukrainian р. Pull the tip of your tongue back without touching anything, and round your lips a little.
  • You said l: your tongue touched the roof of your mouth. For r, the tip of your tongue stays free, pulled back.
 OKChange
ng as in singsinger, ringing, hanging, bringing, sing, thing н (банк) Use the н you say in банк (before к), then stop right there. Sing ends with that sound, and there is no г at the end.
  • You said n: your tongue tip touched behind your teeth. For ng, the back of your tongue lifts to the roof, like the н in банк.
 OKChange
a as in catapple, at, add, ask, cat, bad е (мене) It sits between the Ukrainian е and а. Say е (as in мене), then open your mouth wider, as for а, and keep your tongue forward.
  • You said e (as in bed). Open your mouth wider, as for а.

  • You said a (as in cat). Open your mouth less, like the Ukrainian е.

i as in sitit, in, if, is, sit, ship и (шип) Like the Ukrainian и in шип (ship is almost шип). It is short and relaxed, with your lips loose.
  • You said the long ee of sheep. Relax your lips and make it short, like the Ukrainian и.

  • You said the short i of ship. Smile, and hold it a little longer, like the Ukrainian і.

  • You said the long oo of food. Relax your lips and make it short.

  • You glided at the end. Caught is a pure vowel, like the о in сон: keep your lips still.

the small uhabout, above, again, away, today, lemon no match In a weak syllable, English relaxes the vowel into a short uh in the middle of your mouth. Don't say a clear а or о: make it quick and lazy. OKChange
