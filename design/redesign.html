Articulate, easier and nicer to look at

Rough drafts in the Ukrainian blue and yellow. The words and numbers are made up. Three directions, each shown as three screens that follow one practice from start to finish. They can be mixed.

What makes it hard today

Every screen looks the sameA blue band, white cards, small gray capitals, and rows of pale buttons that all look equal. Nothing says "do this next."
One word takes a long tripPick it in Words, go to Practice, choose Read, Listen, or Speak, then Record. The two games are three taps deep, under Progress.
The same thing, twicePractice buttons on every word in the list, and another on the word's page. The two routes go to the same place.
Small and crowded at the topMost text is 13 to 15 pixels, everything sits in the top half, and the bottom half of the phone is empty.

In all three

  • One big button per screen, the yellow microphone, in reach of a thumb. Everything else is smaller and quieter.
  • Bigger, clearer type: Atkinson Hyperlegible, made to be easy to read, with words at 64 to 76 pixels. No gray capitals.
  • Quiet mode instead of Read · Listen · Speak: one switch for "can't talk right now", which you asked for at the start.
  • The mouth pictures, the boards, and the colors on each part all stay. The other six looks would follow the same layout.

AWord first

Your words are the home screen. Tap one and everything for that word is on one screen: hear it, say it, see how to make it. Two tabs instead of three, and no Read · Listen · Speak switch: quiet mode swaps the microphone for listening and puzzles.

BFive minutes a day

One big button. The app plans a short practice from your words and your hardest sound: listen, say, fix, repeat. You never have to decide what to do next, which helps on a tired day. Words and Progress are still there for when you want them.

CSounds first

Built around the sounds that are hard, with the mouth picture as the star. Each sound has its own page: how to make it, starting from Ukrainian, then a way to say it and a way to hear it. Your words show up under the sounds they need.

Not in these drafts yet: the other six looks, a computer-width layout, and how Fix this part, the boards, and Your colors fit each direction. Most of the screens that change are the teammate's (Read, Listen, Speak), so a redesign needs their OK and their help.
