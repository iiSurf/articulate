Articulate: the first visit

Rough drafts of the tutorial you picked. The first visit teaches by doing: your friend practices their own first word, one step at a time, and finishes having done it, not just read about it. After that, each other screen explains itself once, with a tip the first time it opens.

The card at the bottom of each step says what to do next, and a ring shows where to tap. Skip the tour is always there. The phones use the Sky over wheat look, and one uses Night flag. Tell me what to change by number.
