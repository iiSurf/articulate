Articulate: pictures of how to make each sound

Your friend's idea: show where the tongue goes, not only the lips, because repeating what you hear isn't enough. These are rough drafts. Each picture is one drawing that moves: the tongue, lips, jaw, and the soft palate change for each sound, so all 22 sounds (and the Ukrainian sounds the tips start from) can use it.

Like the teacher does: start from a Ukrainian sound that is already easy, and show the move from there. Not checked yet: the shapes are mine, from general phonetics references. Their teacher would be the perfect person to check them.

Four ways to draw it

All four can be mixed: A and B for consonants, C when the lips matter, D for vowels.

1A: Side view, numbered

A cut-through view of the head, like your friend's teacher would draw: tongue, teeth, lips, roof of the mouth, and the throat. Numbers on the picture match three short steps.

  2. 2Blow air out softly, over your tongue.

4D: A map for vowels

Vowels are about where the tongue's highest point sits. A simple map of the mouth (front on the left, like the side view) puts the English vowels next to the Ukrainian ones. Here: ship and sheep.

ArticulateUA → EN
ship or sheep?

іUkrainian English

ship sits right by the Ukrainian и. For sheep, lift your tongue higher and further forward, to the і spot, smile, and hold it a little longer.
