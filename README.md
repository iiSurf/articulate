# Articulate

A pronunciation practice app for one person: a friend who grew up speaking Ukrainian, speaks English very well, and wants to sound closer to an American accent. They add the words they want to work on, and Articulate suggests similar words to practice alongside them. Then they practice in whichever way fits where they are:

- **Quiet** (on a bus, in a quiet room): puzzles on screen that train the ear in your head. No sound.
- **Headphones:** listen to each word, normal and slow, and pick which word you heard. No speaking.
- **Out loud:** say the word and see each part of it turn green, yellow, orange, or red. Then practice that one part with words that share its sound, and go back to the word.

Built by a team of two with Claude Code for TCSS 490, Artifact 4 ("For Someone Else").

## Privacy

Everything the learner adds (their words and their recordings) stays in the browser on their own phone. No accounts, no server, no analytics, and no paid services.
